Angles and parallel lines

Alternate (Z)góc so le trong

Alternate angles are equal.

Corresponding (F)góc đồng vị

Corresponding angles are equal.

Co-interior (C)góc trong cùng phía

a + b = 180°

!

Watch out

Co-interior angles are not equal

WRONG
x = 65°
co-interior angles are equal

One angle is acute and the other is obtuse, so they cannot be equal.

RIGHT
x + 65° = 180°
x = 115°

Co-interior angles add up to 180°.

★
How to fix it

Z and F give equal angles. C gives two angles that add up to 180°. If one is acute and one is obtuse, add them to 180°.
