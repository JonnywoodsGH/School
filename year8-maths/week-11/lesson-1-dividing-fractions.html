Watch out

Flipping the wrong fraction

WRONG
34 ÷ 25 = 43 × 25
= 815

Flipped the first fraction. The answer is smaller than 34, but dividing by 25 should make it bigger.

RIGHT
34 ÷ 25 = 34 × 52
= 158 = 178

Keep the first. Flip only the one you divide by.

★
How to fix it

Keep, change, flip — the first fraction never moves. Then check: ÷ by less than 1 gives a bigger answer.

Bigger Or Smaller?

Predict before you divide

÷ a proper fraction
8 ÷ 12 = 16
6 ÷ 34 = 8
10 ÷ 25 = 25
✓  answer is bigger
÷ a mixed or improper fraction
8 ÷ 113 = 6
6 ÷ 32 = 4
10 ÷ 212 = 4
→  answer is smaller
★
KEY IDEA

Dividing by less than 1 makes it bigger. Dividing by more than 1 makes it smaller. The opposite of multiplying.
